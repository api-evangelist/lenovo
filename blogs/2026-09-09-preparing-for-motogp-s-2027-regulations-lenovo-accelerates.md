The new 2027 MotoGP regulations mark the biggest technical reset in MotoGP in over a decade. With the introduction of 850cc engines, reduced aerodynamics, no ride-height devices allowed, the introduction of 100% sustainable fuel, and a new tire supplier, Ducati Corse redesigned the Desmosedici GP bike from the ground up.
